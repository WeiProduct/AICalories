Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

AI Calories is built by WeiProduct to help users estimate nutrition, track meals, and review daily health habits. We aim to keep data use clear and limited to product functionality.

Information Processed

  • Profile settings such as nutrition goals or preferences.
  • Meal records, nutrition estimates, and water intake records.
  • Food photos when the user chooses AI recognition.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide nutrition estimates, display history and progress, personalize goals, and improve the user experience.

Third-Party Processing

When AI recognition is used, food photos may be processed by an AI service for analysis. Photos are used for the requested recognition workflow and should not be treated as medical records.

Health Disclaimer

Nutrition estimates are informational and may be inaccurate. They are not medical advice. Users with medical or dietary needs should consult a qualified professional.
